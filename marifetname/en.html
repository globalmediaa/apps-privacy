Marifetname Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains how Marifetname, an iPhone and iPad app developed by Global Media, handles personal data.

In short

Marifetname does not collect any personal data. The app does not connect to the internet, does not ask for an account, shows no ads and does not track you.

What stays on your device

While you use the app, the following is saved only on your device, in the app's own folder:

  • Bookmarks you add
  • Your reading position
  • Your text size and dark theme setting

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot access it. Deleting the app deletes this information. If iCloud or computer backups are turned on for your device, Apple's backup system may include the app's data along with your other apps.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports. The app contains no advertising, analytics, social media or payment services. It does not ask for camera, microphone, location or similar permissions.

The book text

The book text is built into the app. Reading and searching happen entirely on your device; your search words are not sent anywhere.

Apple's services

When you download the app from the App Store, Apple may process some information under its own privacy policy (for example, download counts or, if you allow it, crash reports). That processing is Apple's responsibility.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If we ever add a feature that collects data, we will update this policy beforehand and show the new date on this page.
