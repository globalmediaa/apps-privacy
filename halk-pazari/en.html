Halk Pazarı Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · iOS version 0.5.3 (17) · Türkçe

Halk Pazarı is a fruit and vegetable market game developed by Global Media. This policy explains what data the iOS version of the game on the App Store processes.

Data sent to our server

Other players' stalls also appear in the game's market. To make this work, the game connects to Global Media's server (halkpazari.gmgaming.app) when it starts and sends the following:

  • A random guest player ID and session key that the server assigns automatically. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • The name you give your stall. Other players can see it in the market, so please do not use your real name. Names containing profanity or offensive words are not accepted and are shown to other players as "Tezgâh #NN" (stall number).
  • Your stall positions in the market, your level, the kinds of produce on your stall, your stall's look (canopy, cold cabinet, helper, manager) and whether you are currently playing.

Your full save (cash, stock, ledger) stays on your device. Account linking and cloud save are not available in this version. If they are switched on later, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that happens.

This data is used only to run the game. It is not sold, not used for advertising or tracking, and not shared with third parties. The connection is encrypted with HTTPS.

Reporting and blocking

If you see a stall with an offensive name, you can report it by tapping the stall or from Settings > Tezgâh şikâyet ve engelleri. A report sends the following to our server: your guest player ID, the reported stall's player ID, the stall number, the name you saw, the reason you chose and the date. If the server does not support reports yet, the game opens an email draft with the same details addressed to info@globalmedia.com.tr; sending it is up to you, and in that case we also see your email address. Reports are used only to review abuse, are reviewed within 24 hours, and offensive names are removed. A name reported by several players stays hidden in the market until it is reviewed.

When you block a stall, its name is hidden only on your device. The block list stays on your device, is not sent to our server and can be cleared in Settings.

Data we do not collect

The iOS version has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting tools. The game does not track you across apps or websites and does not access the advertising identifier. It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. All in-game money and loans are virtual.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game shows a single local reminder on your device when you have not played for a day. The reminder is scheduled on the device and nothing is sent to our server.

IP address and server logs

The game server does not store your IP address in its database; it only uses it briefly to limit requests and prevent abuse. The web server in front of it keeps standard access logs (IP address, date and time, requested address) for security and troubleshooting and deletes them after a short period.

Retention and deletion

Your guest ID, stall data and any reports you sent or that were made about your stall stay on the server until you ask us to delete them. The session key is valid for 30 days. Nightly database backups are kept for up to 30 days.

Deleting the app removes the save on your device. To have your stall data removed from the server, email info@globalmedia.com.tr with your in-game stall name and we will complete the request within 30 days. More details: halkpazari.gmgaming.app/hesap-silme.

Children

The game is meant for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
