Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?
No. The whole text is installed with the app, so reading, search and the glossary work offline.

Where are my bookmarks kept?
Only on your device. There is no account or cloud sync, so they do not move to other devices, and deleting the app deletes them.

How do I make the text larger or use the dark theme?
In the reader, tap the “Tт” button at the top right to change text size, line spacing and theme.

How do I look up a word?
Select the word while reading and tap “Kelimeyi açıkla”, or open the glossary with the book icon at the top.

Why are Arabic passages shown as images?
To keep the script intact, these passages show an image of the source page. Tap “Büyüt” to zoom in.

What language is the app in?
The app and the book are in Turkish.
