Akaryakıt Krallığı Privacy Policy

Developer: Global Media · Last updated: 11 October 2026 · Türkçe

This page explains in plain words what the iOS game Akaryakıt Krallığı does with personal data.

In short

The game does not collect any data from you and does not send data off your device. No account is needed and the game does not connect to the internet.

Data we do not collect

  • Contact details such as name, email or phone number
  • Location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data
  • Advertising identifier or device identifier
  • Usage statistics, analytics or crash reports

The game has no ads, no analytics tools, no in-app purchases and no tracking. The in-game "Reklam" (advertising) setting is a virtual expense paid with game money; it does not show real ads.

Information stored on your device

So that you do not lose your progress, the game keeps some information only on your device:

  • Game save: cash, stock, prices, buildings, the name you gave your station, your settings (theme, music, game speed) and in-game statistics
  • Reminder settings: the reminder text, its timing and the status of the notification permission

This information never reaches us. Deleting the game also deletes the save on your device. If iCloud or computer backup is turned on, the save may be included in that backup through Apple's own system; we have no access to these backups.

Reminder notification

After the guide is finished and you have served your first customer, iOS asks for permission to send notifications. If you allow it, your device itself shows a single reminder 24 hours after you leave the game, and the reminder is removed when you open the game again. The notification is scheduled entirely on your device; no server or push service is used. You can turn it off at any time in iPhone Settings > Notifications.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If a feature that collects data is ever added, this page and the privacy details on the App Store will be updated first.
